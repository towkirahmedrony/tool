# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March, 2026

Welcome to **Porokh**. Protecting your private information is our priority. This Privacy Policy applies to the Porokh application and platform, governing data collection, usage, and protection. By using Porokh, you consent to the data practices described in this statement.

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide and improve our educational services, we collect the following types of information:

- **Information You Provide:** Your name, email address, profile picture, and basic information when you register.

- **Authentication Data:** Information securely received when you choose to log in using third-party services like **Google** or **Facebook**.

- **Automatically Collected Data:** Information about how you interact with our platform, including device information and crash logs.

- **App Activity:** Your exam scores, progress, leaderboard rankings, and learning history.

## 2. How We Use Your Data

We collect and use your personal information strictly to:

- Create, operate, and maintain your account securely.
- Deliver educational content, exams, and personalized progress tracking.
- Manage our leaderboard and community features.
- Understand user experiences to fix bugs and improve platform quality.
- Send essential notifications regarding your account or platform updates.

## 3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to ensure a seamless experience. These services follow strict data protection guidelines:

- **Authentication:** Google and Facebook for secure social login.
- **Backend & Database:** We use industry-standard cloud services for secure data storage, analytics, and push notifications.
- We **do not** sell, rent, or share your personal information with ad agencies or unauthorized third parties.

## 4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use local storage and secure session tokens to keep you logged in and manage your preferences seamlessly across sessions.

## 5. Data Security

We secure your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS/SSL encryption, to protect your data during transmission and storage.

## 6. Data Retention & Account Deletion (User Rights)

You have full control over your data. You have the right to:

- **Access and Update:** Modify your personal information at any time from your profile settings.
- **Account Deletion:** You can request to delete your account and associated data directly from the App Settings or by contacting us.
- **Data Retention:** If you delete your account, your personal data will be held for **30 days** for backup and fraud prevention purposes, after which it will be permanently deleted from our active servers.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Porokh is designed for students. However, we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 without verifiable parental consent. If we become aware that we have collected such data, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

## 8. Legal Compliance

We may disclose your personal information if required to do so by law or in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to comply with legal processes or protect the rights and safety of Porokh and its users.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

Porokh reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy. We will notify you about significant changes by sending a notice to your primary email address or placing a prominent notice within the app.
