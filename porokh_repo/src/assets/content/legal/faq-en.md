# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

We have created this page to quickly resolve any of your questions regarding **Porokh**. If you can't find the answer you're looking for, feel free to [contact](/contact) us.

### 1. What exactly is Porokh?
Porokh is a cutting-edge educational technology (EdTech) platform and a dedicated online examination web application. Our mission is to cultivate a reliable, accessible, and comprehensive exam-preparation ecosystem for students nationwide, featuring real-time online exams, board question analytics, and standardized model tests. To learn more about our platform's vision, visit the [About Us](/about) page.

### 2. Can I share my premium account with friends?
No, your premium account is strictly for personal use. Sharing login credentials or account access is prohibited by our policy and may result in account termination. To learn more about general platform usage guidelines, read our [Terms & Conditions](/terms-conditions).

### 3. Will I get a refund if I cancel my subscription?
All payments for subscriptions or services at Porokh are **non-refundable**. Details about our payment policy are mentioned on our [Terms & Conditions](/terms-conditions) page.

### 4. How secure is my personal information and exam data?
Protecting your private information is our priority. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with ad agencies or unauthorized third parties. To know how we protect your data privacy, you can check our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) page.

### 5. What happens to my data if I want to delete my account?
You have full control over your data and can request account deletion at any time. However, if you delete your account, your personal data will be held for **30 days** for backup and fraud prevention purposes, after which it will be permanently deleted from our active servers. Read our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) for more details.

### 6. Can I use Porokh if I block browser cookies?
You can modify your browser settings to decline or block cookies if you prefer. However, please be aware that if you choose to disable or block essential cookies, you may not be able to access certain features of Porokh, such as logging into your account or participating in live exams. To understand why and what type of cookies we use, visit our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy).

### 7. Is Porokh affiliated with any government education board?
No, Porokh operates as an independent educational entity. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government education board, ministry, or formal examination authority. To learn more about this, visit our [About Us](/about) page.
