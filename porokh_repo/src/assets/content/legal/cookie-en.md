# Cookie Policy

At **Porokh**, we use cookies and similar tracking technologies to ensure our platform is secure, efficient, and tailored to provide you with the best possible user experience. By continuing to use our website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

## 1. What are Cookies?
Cookies are small pieces of text data stored on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) by your web browser when you visit a website. These files allow the website to recognize your device and remember specific information about your visit and preferences over time.

## 2. Why Do We Use Cookies?
Cookies play a crucial role in delivering a seamless and optimized experience on the Porokh platform. We use them primarily to:
* Keep you signed in and maintain your active session when you return to the platform.
* Enhance the overall speed, performance, and security of our website.
* Understand user behavior and analyze traffic patterns to continuously improve our services.
* Remember your localized preferences, such as language settings (Bengali/English) or display themes.

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use
To provide our core services effectively, we categorize the cookies we use into the following types:

### A. Essential Cookies
These cookies are strictly necessary for the fundamental operation of the website. They enable core functionalities like user authentication, secure account access, and fraud prevention. Without these cookies, the basic features of Porokh cannot function properly.

### B. Analytics Cookies
Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our platform. They collect anonymous data on metrics such as the number of visitors, page views, and navigation patterns. This information is vital for troubleshooting issues and optimizing the platform's educational tools.

### C. Preference Cookies
Preference cookies allow the website to remember the choices you have made during past visits. This includes remembering your preferred language, region, or customized UI settings (like Dark Mode), ensuring a personalized experience every time you log in.

## 4. Third-Party Cookies
In addition to our first-party cookies, we may utilize trusted third-party services (such as Google Analytics) to monitor and analyze website performance. These third parties may also place cookies on your device, which are governed by their respective privacy policies.

## 5. How to Manage or Disable Cookies
You have full control over your cookie preferences and can modify them at any time through your browser settings.
* **Browser Configuration:** Most web browsers (such as Chrome, Firefox, Safari) automatically accept cookies, but you can usually modify your browser settings to decline cookies if you prefer.
* **Important Note:** Please be aware that if you choose to disable or block essential cookies, you may not be able to access certain features of Porokh, such as logging into your account or participating in live exams.
