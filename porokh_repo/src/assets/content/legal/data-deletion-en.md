# Data Deletion Instructions

**Effective Date:** March 2026

At **Porokh**, we respect your privacy and give you control over your personal data. If you want to delete your account and the personal data associated with it, you can do so by following the instructions below.

## Method 1: Delete Your Account in the App (Recommended)

You can request account deletion directly from within the Porokh app:

1. Log in to your Porokh account.
2. Go to the **Settings** page.
3. Scroll down to the **Account Management** section.
4. Tap or click **Delete Account**.
5. Review the confirmation message and confirm your request.

## Method 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access your account or prefer manual assistance, you may request deletion by email:

1. Send an email to **[info.parapoth@gmail.com](mailto:info.parapoth@gmail.com)** from the email address associated with your Porokh account.
2. Use the subject line: **Data Deletion Request - [Your Name]**
3. In the email body, clearly state that you want your account and associated personal data deleted.
4. Our support team will review and process your request within **3-5 business days**.

## What Happens When You Request Deletion?

As described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy):

- **Account Access:** Once your deletion request is confirmed, your account may be deactivated or scheduled for deletion.
- **Retention Period:** Certain data may be retained for a limited period where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or dispute resolution.
- **Permanent Deletion:** After the applicable retention period, your personal data will be permanently deleted or anonymized from our active systems, unless a longer retention period is required by law.
- **Third-Party Login Providers:** If you signed in using Google or Facebook, deleting your Porokh account will remove your account from our platform. You may also revoke Porokh's access at any time from your Google or Facebook account settings.
